Programme

8 days among the most famous fjords

Day01

Meet-up in Bergen and the first villa

  • A scenic drive from Bergen to a premium villa
  • Introductions and dinner with a fjord view
Day02

The Troll’s Tongue — Trolltunga

  • Trolltunga hike from the upper car park with a guide
  • Epic photos on the cliff edge above the fjord
  • Unwinding in a private sauna and jacuzzi
Day03

Vøringsfossen waterfall and the Queen’s trail

  • The Hardanger Bridge and viewing platforms over the cascading Vøringsfossen
  • The panoramic Dronningstien trail with a bird’s-eye view of the fjords
  • Sauna and jacuzzi at the villa
Day04

Emerald Lake Bondhusvatnet

  • An easy forest walk to Lake Bondhusvatnet
  • The Bondhusbreen glacier tongue — atmospheric shots without the strain
  • Lunch at a local spot
Day05

The road of waterfalls and the drive south

  • A scenic drive with stops at waterfalls
  • Check-in at the second villa near Stavanger
Day06

The legendary Preikestolen cliff

  • An early start, before the crowds
  • A trek to the flat Preikestolen rock, 604 metres above Lysefjord
  • Lunch at a local spot, dinner at the villa
Day07

Kjeragbolten and the farewell dinner

  • Up to the Kjerag plateau at the head of Lysefjord
  • A trek to Kjeragbolten, a boulder wedged in a crevice almost 1,000 metres up
  • Farewell dinner at the villa
Day08

Departure

  • Signature breakfast and trip wrap-up
  • Transfer to Stavanger airport, flight home
