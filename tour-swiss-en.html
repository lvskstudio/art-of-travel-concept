Programme

7 days in the heart of the Alps

Day01

Arrival and an evening in Grindelwald

  • A scenic drive through alpine landscapes to Grindelwald
  • Check-in at a premium private chalet
  • First dinner and introductions
Day02

Lauterbrunnen, the valley of waterfalls, and Mürren

  • Lauterbrunnen canyon with dozens of waterfalls
  • A cable car to the quiet car-free village of Mürren
  • A walk along scenic paths, lunch on a waterside terrace
Day03

Grindelwald First, Cliff Walk and Lake Bachalpsee

  • An early cable car up to First while the peaks are quiet
  • The Cliff Walk suspension bridge over the abyss
  • A trek to mirror-like Lake Bachalpsee, lunch on a mountain terrace
Day04

“Top of Europe” — Jungfraujoch

  • A mountain train to Europe’s highest railway station (3,454 m)
  • The Sphinx terrace and a view of the Aletsch Glacier
  • Ice tunnels and the Ice Palace, signature dinner
Day05

Iseltwald, Lake Brienz and Harder Kulm

  • Iseltwald village and its famous wooden pier on a turquoise lake
  • Lunch with a panoramic view
  • The Harder Kulm viewpoint at golden hour
Day06

Castles of Lake Thun and the farewell dinner

  • Oberhofen Castle on Lake Thun
  • Interlaken and the best Swiss chocolate
  • Farewell dinner at the chalet
Day07

Transfer and departure

  • Signature breakfast and trip wrap-up
  • Transfer to the airport, flight home
