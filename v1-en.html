Hi, I’m Artem —
let’s get acquainted!

I was born in Shepetivka, a small town in Ukraine. My love of travel showed early: I remember being six on a 14-hour drive to the sea, refusing to sleep and sitting up front next to my dad the whole way — watching the road and enjoying the moment.

At 19 I decided to fly across the ocean on my own and try living in Canada — without speaking the language. First I figured things out for myself, and later I helped others settle in too.

RESTLESSNESS · COURAGE · ALWAYS UP FOR IT · RESOURCEFULNESS
ORGANISATION · RESPONSIBILITY · DISCIPLINE · SENSE OF HUMOUR

In Canada I realised I love the mountains.

In Mexico — that I’m crazy about the sea.

In Madeira — that nothing beats the two together.
